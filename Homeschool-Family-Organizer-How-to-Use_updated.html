🏠 Homeschool Family Organizer - Complete User Guide

Everything you need to organize, track, and document your family's homeschool journey.

🚀 Getting Started

Opening the App

  1. Double-click "HomeschoolFamilyOrganizer.html"
  2. Browser opens automatically
  3. App loads with sample family data
  4. Start organizing your homeschool

First Time Setup

  1. Add Students: Enter all children's info
  2. Add Subjects: List subjects they're studying
  3. Log Daily Learning: Track each day's activities
  4. Record Grades: Document assessments
  5. View Progress: See student progress reports

🧭 How the App Works

The easiest way to use the organizer is to follow this order:

  1. Students: Add each child you are homeschooling.
  2. Curriculum: Add the subjects each child is studying and the curriculum or objectives for each subject.
  3. Daily Log: Select a student and one of that student's subjects, then record the date, time, and what was learned.
  4. Grades: Select the same student and subject when recording assessments.
  5. Progress: Review the averages and subject summaries generated from the grades you entered.
💡 Simple rule: Set up Students and Subjects first. Daily Logs and Grades then build the records shown in the Dashboard and Progress sections.

📊 Dashboard Tab

Overview Metrics

  • Students: Total children in your homeschool
  • Subjects: Different subjects being taught
  • Learning Days: Unique calendar dates with at least one learning log
  • Avg Grade: Average grade across all assessments

Quick Actions

  • + Add Student (new child)
  • + Add Subject (new subject area)
  • + Log Today (daily learning log)
  • View All (see complete lists)

Active Subjects Overview

See active subjects with progress bars based on the recorded average grades for each student and subject.

👨‍👩‍👧 Students Tab

Adding Students

  1. Click "+ Add Student"
  2. Enter full name
  3. Select grade level
  4. Enter age
  5. Set start date (when began homeschool)
  6. Click "Add Student"

Why This Matters

Tracking all children in one place ensures no subject or assessment is forgotten. Perfect for families with multiple children at different levels.

💡 Tip: Add all children at the beginning of the school year. This makes organizing subjects and tracking progress easy.

📚 Curriculum Tab

Adding Subjects

  1. Click "+ Add Subject"
  2. Enter subject name (Math, Reading, Science, etc.)
  3. Select which student is studying it
  4. Select grade level
  5. Enter curriculum used (Saxon, Singapore, Classical, etc.)
  6. Write learning objectives
  7. Click "Add Subject"

Curriculum Ideas

  • Mathematics (specify curriculum)
  • English Language Arts
  • Science
  • Social Studies / History
  • Foreign Language
  • Art
  • Music
  • Physical Education
💡 Tip: Include the specific curriculum you're using. This documents your educational approach and is helpful for record-keeping.

📅 Daily Log Tab

Why Track Daily Learning?

Many states require documentation of homeschool activities. Daily logs provide proof of educational engagement and help you see patterns in learning.

Logging Daily Activities

  1. Click "+ Log Today's Learning"
  2. Select date
  3. Select student
  4. Select the student first, then select one of that student’s subjects
  5. Enter duration (minutes)
  6. Add notes about activities
  7. Click "Log Activity"

What to Include

  • Math: "Completed Chapter 5 in Saxon Math, 20 problems"
  • Reading: "Read chapters 3-5 of novel, discussed comprehension questions"
  • Science: "Conducted plant growth experiment, measured height"
  • History: "Studied American Revolution, created timeline"
  • Art: "Learned watercolor techniques, created landscape"
💡 Pro Tip: Log at the end of each day while activities are fresh. Include specific details about what was learned or accomplished.

📈 Grades Tab

Recording Assessments

  1. Click "+ Record Grade"
  2. Select student
  3. Select one of that student’s subjects
  4. Name the assignment or test
  5. Enter grade (0-100)
  6. Set date
  7. Add optional notes
  8. Click "Record Grade"

Types of Assessments

  • Quizzes (quick checks of understanding)
  • Tests (comprehensive assessments)
  • Projects (completed assignments)
  • Essays (writing assessments)
  • Lab Reports (science documentation)
  • Participation (engagement and effort)

Grading Tips

  • Be consistent with grading scales
  • Document major assessments
  • Record both strengths and areas for growth
  • Include dates for tracking progress over time

📥 📤 Export & Backup

Exporting Your Data

  1. Click "📤 Export" button
  2. CSV file downloads
  3. Save to Google Drive for backup
  4. Export monthly or quarterly

CSV Includes

  • All student information
  • All subjects and curricula
  • Complete daily learning logs
  • All grades and assessments

Importing a Backup

  1. Click "📥 Import CSV" in the app header
  2. Select a CSV previously exported from Homeschool Family Organizer
  3. Confirm that you want to replace the current data
  4. The students, subjects, daily logs, and grades are restored
⚠️ Important: Import replaces the current data in the app. Export a backup first if you want to keep the current records.

Resetting the App

The "🔄 Reset" button clears the current students, subjects, daily logs, and grades after confirmation. Export a backup before using Reset if you may need the data later.

Backup Strategy

  • Export every month
  • Save to Google Drive
  • Keep yearly archives
  • Never lose important records

📋 Homeschool Record Keeping

What the App Records

  • ✅ Student information
  • ✅ Subjects and curriculum details
  • ✅ Daily learning activities and time spent
  • ✅ Grades and assessments
  • ✅ Progress summaries based on recorded grades

Important Note About Learning Days

The Dashboard's "Learning Days" metric counts unique calendar dates that contain at least one Daily Log. It is a learning-activity metric, not a formal attendance tracker.

Using Your Records

  1. Keep daily learning logs throughout the school year
  2. Record important assessments and grades
  3. Review Progress reports periodically
  4. Export your records quarterly or annually
  5. Keep backups in a safe location
💡 Important: Homeschool record-keeping requirements vary by state and situation. Check your state's current requirements and use this app as an organizational record-keeping tool, not as legal advice or a guarantee of compliance.

❓ FAQ

Q: Can I track multiple children?

Answer: Yes! Add each child as a separate student. The app handles unlimited children.

Q: How detailed should my daily logs be?

Answer: Include subject, duration, and what was accomplished. This proves educational engagement.

Q: Can I change grades after entering them?

Answer: Delete the entry and re-enter with the correct grade. It takes 30 seconds.

Q: Is this data private?

Answer: Your homeschool records are stored locally in your browser and are not uploaded to a homeschool account or server by the app. Exported files are under your control.

Q: Can I use this for transcripts?

Answer: The app records grades and progress information that can be exported and used as supporting records when preparing a transcript. It does not generate an official transcript.

Q: What if I need to prove education records?

Answer: Export your complete records (daily logs + grades). This documents the entire school year.

Q: Does it work offline?

Answer: Yes. The core app works locally in your browser and does not require an account or internet connection to record and manage your homeschool data.
